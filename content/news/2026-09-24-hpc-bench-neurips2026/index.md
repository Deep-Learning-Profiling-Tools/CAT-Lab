Our paper [*HPC-Bench: A Comprehensive Benchmark for High Performance Computing Codes*]({{< relref "/publication/HPC-Bench_NeurIPS2026" >}}), by **Bowen Cui, Junyu Yin, Tejas Ramesh, Leo Lim, Oscar Hernandez, and Keren Zhou**, has been accepted in the **NeurIPS 2026 Evaluations and Datasets track**. HPC-Bench evaluates LLM-generated kernel optimizations inside complete HPC programs, combining program-level correctness checks with performance measurements. Congratulations to all the authors!
