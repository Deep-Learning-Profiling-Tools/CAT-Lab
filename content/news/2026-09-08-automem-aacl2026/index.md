Our collaborative paper [*Exploring Cross-Scenario Generality of Agentic Memory Systems: Diagnostics and a Strong Baseline*]({{< relref "/publication/AutoMEM_AACL2026" >}}), by **Zhikai Chen, Jialiang Gu, Junyu Yin, Xianxuan Long, Shenglai Zeng, Xiaoze Liu, Kai Guo, Keren Zhou, and Jiliang Tang**, has been accepted at **AACL-IJCNLP 2026**. The paper evaluates agent memory systems across different scenarios and introduces AutoMEM, a baseline that lets agents manage memory through tools. Congratulations to all the authors!
