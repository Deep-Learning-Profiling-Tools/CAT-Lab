Our paper [*NKI Teaching Lab: An Accelerator-Free Toolchain for Learning Tile-Level AI Accelerator Programming*]({{< relref "/publication/NKILab_SIGCSE2027" >}}), by **Jialiang Gu, Junjie Ma, Thai-Hoa Nguyen, Hao Wu, Zhicong Lu, Shuyin Jiao, Binqian Yin, and Keren Zhou**, has been accepted at **SIGCSE TS 2027**, the ACM Technical Symposium on Computer Science Education. The teaching lab helps students learn tile-level AI accelerator programming without accelerator hardware. Congratulations to all the authors!
