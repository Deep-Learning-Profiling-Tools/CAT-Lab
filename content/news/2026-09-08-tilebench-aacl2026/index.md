Our paper [*TileBench: A Controlled Benchmark for Performance Evaluation and Bottleneck Diagnosis of Tile-Based Programming Models*]({{< relref "/publication/TileBench_AACL2026" >}}), by **Bowen Cui, Zhongchun Zhou, Hao Wu, Tejas Ramesh, Junyu Yin, Jialiang Gu, and Keren Zhou**, has been accepted at **AACL-IJCNLP 2026**. TileBench compares tile-based GPU programming models under controlled conditions and supports profiling-guided diagnosis of performance bottlenecks. Congratulations to all the authors!
